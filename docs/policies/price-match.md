# Price Match Guarantee

## What we match
NovaMart matches the current advertised price of an **identical item** (same brand, model, size, colour) from these competitors: **ValueHub, MegaShop, and CityMart**, in-store or on their websites.

## What we do not match
- Marketplace / third-party sellers (e.g. sellers on marketplace platforms).
- Clearance, open-box, refurbished, or "limited quantity" offers.
- Bundle deals, membership-only prices, or prices requiring a coupon.
- Black Friday (the Friday after US Thanksgiving) through Cyber Monday: **no price matching**.

## Price adjustments after purchase
If NovaMart lowers its own price within **14 days** of purchase, the customer can request a refund of the difference with the receipt. This does not apply to competitor prices after purchase.

## How to process
The associate verifies the competitor price on the competitor's website on a store device. Matches up to **$100** off can be done by an associate; above $100 off requires a manager.

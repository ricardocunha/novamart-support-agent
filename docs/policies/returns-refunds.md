# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Standard return window
Most items can be returned within **30 days** of the purchase date (in-store) or the **delivery date** (online orders). The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by a receipt or order number.

## Category exceptions
- **Electronics** (phones, laptops, tablets, TVs, consoles): **15-day** return window. Opened electronics incur a **15% restocking fee** unless the item is defective.
- **Perishable groceries**: no returns; offer a refund only for spoiled or recalled items (see Damaged Goods SOP).
- **Final sale / clearance items** (price tag ending in .97): not returnable unless defective.
- **Personal care, earrings, swimwear**: not returnable once the hygiene seal is broken.
- **Gift cards**: never refundable or returnable.

## Refund method
Refunds go back to the **original payment method**. Cash purchases over **$200** are refunded by cheque mailed within 7 business days, not cash at the till. Without a receipt or order number, a store associate may issue **store credit only**, at the item's lowest selling price in the last 30 days, maximum **$50** per customer per day.

## Holiday extension
Items purchased between **November 1 and December 24** may be returned until **January 31** of the following year. The electronics 15-day window still applies to electronics.

## Approvals
- Associates can process any standard return up to **$500**.
- Returns above **$500**, receipt-less returns above $50, and any waiver of the restocking fee require a **store manager override**.
- Customers flagged for return abuse (system message "RETURN LIMIT") cannot be overridden in store; escalate to Ops (Loss Prevention).

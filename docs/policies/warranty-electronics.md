# Warranty & Electronics Service

## Manufacturer warranty
All new electronics include the manufacturer warranty (usually **1 year**). After the 15-day NovaMart return window, defects are handled by the manufacturer. Associates should give the customer the manufacturer's support contact printed on the receipt.

## NovaMart Care+ extended protection
- Sold at checkout for items over **$100**; can be added up to **30 days** after purchase with the receipt.
- Covers defects and accidental damage (drops, spills) for **2 or 3 years**.
- Does **not** cover loss, theft, cosmetic damage that does not affect function, or batteries after year 1.
- Claims: the customer brings the item to any store; the associate opens a Care+ claim in the POS. Repairs take **7–10 business days**; if not repairable, the customer receives a NovaMart gift card for the original purchase price.

## Dead on arrival (DOA)
If an electronic item does not power on within **48 hours** of purchase or delivery, it is DOA: exchange immediately in store for the same model, no restocking fee, no manager approval needed.

## Data and devices
Associates must never ask for a customer's device password. Advise customers to back up and factory-reset devices before returns or repairs.

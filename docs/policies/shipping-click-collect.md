# Shipping & Click-and-Collect

## Shipping options
- **Standard**: 3–5 business days, free over $50 (otherwise $5.99).
- **Express**: 1–2 business days, $14.99.
- Orders are dispatched from the regional warehouse; tracking is sent by email once shipped.

## Delayed orders
An order is **delayed** when it is more than **3 business days** past its estimated delivery date. For delayed orders, the associate can offer a **$10 store credit** (once per order). If an order is more than **10 business days** late, it is treated as lost: the customer can choose a reshipment or a full refund, and the associate must open an Ops ticket (category: shipping).

## Click-and-Collect
- Orders are ready within **2 hours** during store hours when items are in stock at that store; the customer receives an SMS with a pickup code.
- Orders are held for **7 days**. After that, they are cancelled and refunded automatically.
- Pickup requires the pickup code **and** photo ID matching the order name. Someone else can collect only if the customer added an alternate pickup person when ordering.

## Address changes
Delivery addresses cannot be changed in store after an order has shipped. The customer must contact the carrier, or the associate opens an Ops ticket before dispatch.

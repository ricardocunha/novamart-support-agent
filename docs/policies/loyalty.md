# NovaRewards Loyalty Program

## Earning points
Members earn **1 point per $1** spent (before tax). **Gold** members (spending over $1,500 in a calendar year) earn **1.5 points per $1**. Gift card purchases do not earn points.

## Redeeming points
**100 points = $5** reward, issued in $5 increments at checkout. Rewards cannot be redeemed for cash or gift cards.

## Expiry
Points expire **12 months** after they are earned if the account has no activity in that period.

## Returns and points
When an item is returned, the points earned on it are removed. If the customer has already spent those points, the equivalent value is deducted from the refund.

## Missing points
Customers can claim missing points up to **60 days** after purchase with a receipt. Associates can add up to **500 points** manually; larger corrections must go to the CRM team through an Ops ticket.

## Privacy
Associates may confirm a member's points balance only after verifying the member's phone number or email. Never read out the full address or payment details.

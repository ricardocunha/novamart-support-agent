# Damaged Goods & Escalation SOP

## Damaged on delivery
If an online order arrives damaged, the customer must report it within **7 days** of delivery with a photo. The associate can offer a replacement or a full refund without requiring the item back if its value is under **$75**. Above $75, the damaged item must be returned in store or collected by the carrier.

## Damaged in store
Items damaged on the shop floor are moved to the damages cage, scanned as "DMG" in the handheld, and never re-shelved.

## Recalled products
When a product is recalled, customers get a full refund **with or without a receipt**, regardless of the return window. Spoiled perishable items get a refund or replacement with proof of purchase.

## Safety incidents
If a product caused injury or a safety hazard (e.g. a battery overheating), stop the sale of that SKU in the store, do not discard the item, and escalate **immediately** to Ops (category: safety, urgency: high). Do not admit liability to the customer.

## When to escalate to Ops
Open an Ops ticket when:
- the answer is not covered by policy,
- an action needs approval above store-manager level,
- an order is lost, mis-shipped, or charged incorrectly,
- a customer is flagged for return abuse,
- a safety incident occurs.
Include the order number, what the customer asked for, the policy you checked, and what you have already done.
